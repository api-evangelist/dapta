# Dapta AI

> No-code AI voice and text agent platform for SMBs. Build AI phone agents, SMS and WhatsApp bots, meeting notetakers, and workflow automations in minutes without writing code. Trusted by 50,000+ companies.

## Products

- [AI Voice Agents](https://dapta.ai/ai-voice-agent/): AI phone agents that answer inbound calls, make outbound calls, qualify leads, book appointments, and handle support 24/7 in 20+ languages.
- [AI Text Agents (SMS)](https://dapta.ai/ai-text-agent-for-ai-sms/): Automate SMS conversations for lead nurturing, appointment reminders, follow-ups, and support with natural AI responses.
- [WhatsApp AI Agents](https://dapta.ai/whatsapp-ai-agents/): Automated WhatsApp Business conversations that answer, qualify, and push every message to your CRM 24/7.
- [AI Notetaker](https://dapta.ai/ai-notetaker/): AI meeting recorder and summarizer for Zoom, Google Meet, and Microsoft Teams with transcripts, summaries, and action items.
- [Workflow Automation](https://dapta.ai/workflow-automation/): Visual no-code automations connecting 1000+ tools, with built-in AI capabilities.
- [AI Agent Campaigns](https://dapta.ai/ai-agent-campaigns/): Run outbound AI call campaigns at scale from uploaded contact lists and scripts.
- [Dapti AI Agent](https://dapta.ai/dapti-ai-agent/): Dapta's customizable AI agent for tailored voice and text automation use cases.
